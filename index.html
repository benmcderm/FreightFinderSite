What is {{BRAND}}?

{{BRAND}} is a load board app for truckers, owner-operators and small fleets. Instead of scrolling a website built for a desk, you open the app in the cab and see the loads that are actually near your truck: on a map, or in a list sorted by distance, best rate, or estimated profit. Every load shows the equipment type, weight, pickup date, posted rate and rate per mile, and a Call Now button that dials the shipper or broker who posted it.

The board is built from loads that shippers and brokers post publicly, refreshed every hour, with phone numbers and pickup coordinates filled in so you spend your time calling, not searching. {{BRAND}} is not a broker and never takes a cut. Features

  • Loads near me — a live map clusters every load around your location; zoom in and tap a pin for the details. How it works.
  • Sorted by distance — nearest pickups first, so you cut deadhead miles instead of reading every post.
  • Van, flatbed, reefer — one tap filters the board to your trailer; step deck, drop deck and specialized loads are included too.
  • Rate per mile — see $/mi on every load with a posted rate, and a conservative estimate when there isn't one. Read the guide.
  • Estimated profit — enter your operating cost per mile and sort by what a load actually leaves you.
  • Call Now — the poster's phone number is on the load; one tap to call and book direct.
  • Daily load alerts — a push notification each day with fresh loads within 50, 100, 200 or 300 miles of you.
  • Refreshed hourly — thousands of loads across the US, checked every hour so stale posts drop off.
  • Works offline — the last board is cached, so you can keep reading in dead zones.

How {{BRAND}} works in four steps

  1. Install the app and allow location — no account needed to browse.
  2. Open the Map to see loads around you, or the Loads list sorted by distance, rate or profit.
  3. Filter to your trailer type and tap a load for the route, miles, rate per mile, weight and pickup date.
  4. Tap Call Now, talk to the shipper or broker, and book it.

Frequently asked questions

What is {{BRAND}}?

{{BRAND}} is a load board app for truck drivers and owner-operators. It gathers publicly posted truck loads from across the country, refreshes them every hour, and shows them on a map sorted by how far the pickup is from your truck, with the posted rate, rate per mile, equipment type, weight, pickup date and a Call Now button for the shipper or broker.

Is {{BRAND}} free?

{{BRAND}} is free to download on the App Store and Google Play, and you can browse loads without creating an account. Premium unlocks every load, contact details, nearest-first sorting and priority load alerts for {{PRICE_MONTH}} per month or {{PRICE_YEAR}} per year, and it starts with a free trial.

Where do the loads come from?

{{BRAND}} collects loads that shippers and brokers post publicly on free load boards, adds the pickup location, distance from you, equipment class and phone number, and refreshes the list every hour. When a load has no posted rate, the app shows a conservative estimate based on national average spot rates for that equipment type. More in free load boards for truckers.

Is {{BRAND}} a broker?

No. {{BRAND}} is not a broker and never takes a cut of a load. Tapping Call Now dials the shipper or broker who posted the load, and you negotiate and book directly with them.

Which trucks does {{BRAND}} cover?

Dry van, reefer and flatbed loads are the bulk of the board, with step deck, drop deck, RGN and other specialized equipment included when they are posted. Filters let you show only van, flatbed or reefer loads.
