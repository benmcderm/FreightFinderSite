{{BRAND}} Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026. This policy replaces the earlier version dated March 9, 2025.

{{BRAND}} is a load board app for iPhone and Android made by {{COMPANY}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app and this website collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The short version: the app needs your location to show loads near you, keeps an optional account if you create one, uses a few third-party services to run subscriptions, notifications and advertising measurement, and never sells your personal information.

1. Information the app collects

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device's current location while you are using it, to sort loads by distance from your truck, draw the map around you, and pick which loads go into your daily load alerts. We store only your most recent coordinates and the time they were recorded, in our database, so that alerts can be sent while the app is closed. We do not track your location in the background and we do not keep a history of where you have been. You can turn location off in your device settings at any time; the app then falls back to sorting by rate.

Account information (optional)

You can browse loads without an account. If you create one, we store the email address and password you sign up with (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication and never stored in a form we can read), the date the account was created, your alert preferences, your cost-per-mile setting, your last location, your push notification token, and whether you hold an active subscription.

Anonymous device record

When the app is first installed it generates a random identifier for the device. We use it, whether or not you have an account, to store your push notification token, your last location for alerts, and a few engagement counters (number of sessions, loads viewed, shippers contacted, and whether the review prompt has been shown). This identifier is not derived from your phone's hardware or advertising IDs, and a reinstall generates a new one.

Subscription status

If you subscribe to Premium, Apple or Google process the payment. We never see your card or bank details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous RevenueCat identifier and, if you are signed in, your account identifier, and tells the app whether your subscription is active.

Usage and crash information

The app increments daily aggregate counters (for example how many times the app was opened or a load was viewed across all users) that contain no identifiers. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error in a production build, it records the error message, the screen it came from, your platform (iOS or Android) and the app version. We use this only to fix bugs.

Calls to shippers

Tapping Call Now opens your phone's dialer. We record that a contact was made, for the engagement counters above, but we do not record, listen to, or store the call itself or the number you dialled.

2. How we use information

  • To show loads near you, sorted by distance, rate or estimated profit.
  • To send the daily load alerts you turn on, for the radius you choose.
  • To keep your settings and subscription status in sync across devices when you sign in.
  • To measure, in aggregate, how the app is used and to find and fix crashes.
  • To measure whether our advertising leads to installs and subscriptions (see Meta below).
  • To respond when you contact us.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your location or contact details for advertising.

4. Load postings and shipper information

The loads in the app are postings that shippers and brokers make publicly, including business names and phone numbers. We collect them from public load boards and refresh them every hour. If you are a shipper or broker and want a posting removed or corrected, email us and we will take it down.

5. How long we keep information

  • Account data stays until you delete the account (see section 7).
  • Device records (push token, last location, counters) stay while the device keeps using the app. A device that stops sending updates is left with its last values; email us to have a record removed.
  • Last location is overwritten each time it is updated; we keep only the latest.
  • Crash reports and aggregate counters are kept for troubleshooting and trend analysis and contain no account information.
  • Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat and the app stores for as long as their policies require.

6. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. Our database is protected by access rules so that only your signed-in session can read or change your own account data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that information will never be lost or accessed without authorization.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Location: allow or deny it in your device's settings for {{BRAND}}.
  • Notifications: turn daily load alerts off in the Profile tab, or block notifications in your device settings.
  • Delete your account: Profile tab → Delete Account removes your account data from our database and deletes your sign-in. You can also email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the address you signed up with.
  • Delete your device record: deleting the app stops all updates; to have the stored record erased, email us.
  • Access, correction and portability: email us and we will send you a copy of the information we hold about you or correct it.
  • Subscriptions: cancel any time through your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

Residents of California, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other places with privacy laws have additional rights, including the right to complain to a supervisory authority. We honour these requests for everyone regardless of where you live. We will not treat you differently for exercising them.

9. Children
